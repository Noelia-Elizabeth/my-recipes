# 👩‍🍳 My Recipes Book 📖

Welcome to my digital recipe book! 

I've always loved cooking, but I always ended up losing my favorite recipes among screenshots, phone notes, and loose papers. So, I decided to use my frontend development skills to create my own solution: a beautiful and easy-to-use website where I can check everything I love to cook.

## ✨ What makes this recipe book special?
- **Tailor-made:** Designed exactly how I like to read a recipe.
- **Visual:** Because we eat with our eyes first, every recipe has its featured photo.
- **Always at hand:** Ready to look perfect on both desktop and mobile devices.
